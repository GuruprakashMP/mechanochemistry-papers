About

MechanochemistryPapers is an automatically updated index of mechanochemistry research papers — ball milling and grinding, liquid-assisted grinding, twin-screw extrusion, mechanocatalysis and mechanoredox, cocrystals and pharmaceutical solids, MOF and framework mechanosynthesis, polymer mechanochemistry and mechanophores, piezo- and tribocatalysis, mechanical alloying and in-situ reaction monitoring — covering experimental, computational and data-driven studies alike.

How it works

Every day an automated pipeline queries open scholarly metadata APIs, removes duplicates, classifies each paper with a mechanochemistry relevance model, assigns categories and a relevance score, and regenerates this site.

Data policy

This site stores metadata only: title, authors, journal, date, DOI and link. No abstracts, no full text, no PDFs. Every paper links to the original publisher page. To have a record corrected or removed, contact guruprakash29011999@gmail.com.
